# Atlas privacy

Atlas does not require an account. This version contains no analytics SDK, advertising SDK, advertising identifier collection, tracking pixels, or app-operated backend.

## On your device

Your chosen country, country watchlist, and downloaded country data are stored in the app's private device storage. Clearing Android app storage or uninstalling removes them. Android backup is disabled for this app.

## Network requests

The app contacts the World Bank public API for country metadata and annual statistics and publisher RSS feeds for news. These providers receive the network metadata normally necessary to serve requests, including your IP address. The country you choose determines which data are requested. Their own privacy policies apply.

When you open a source or headline, Android opens an external browser or another installed application. That destination has its own privacy practices. Atlas does not collect your browsing history.

No API keys or personal credentials are needed for this version. Do not embed premium data-provider credentials in an APK; any future paid-provider integration should use a properly secured backend.

## Distribution

This is an early open-source release, not a financial-advice service. Before a store launch, the publisher must review this policy against the shipped binary, provide a real contact address, complete the store Data Safety questionnaire, and publish a stable policy URL.

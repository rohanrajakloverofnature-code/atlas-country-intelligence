# Data and third-party notices

## Official statistical data

Attribution: **The World Bank: World Development Indicators / Worldwide Governance Indicators: original data provider as identified in each indicator’s metadata.** Atlas is an independent application and is not endorsed by the World Bank or its data providers.

Country metadata and annual observations are obtained from the [World Bank Indicators API v2](https://datahelpdesk.worldbank.org/knowledgebase/articles/889392-about-the-indicators-api-documentation). Indicator codes and source links are preserved. The [indicator metadata endpoint](https://datahelpdesk.worldbank.org/knowledgebase/articles/898599-indicator-api-queries) identifies underlying source organizations; the World Bank may be an aggregator rather than the original collector.

World Bank datasets are generally supplied under [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/) with the World Bank’s additional terms, unless labeled otherwise. See the controlling [Terms of Use for Datasets](https://www.worldbank.org/ext/en/legal/terms-conditions/datasets). Downstream recipients must preserve World Bank and provider acknowledgment and applicable dataset terms. Check individual source conditions before commercial redistribution.

Atlas sorts, filters, formats, caches and visualizes observations. It does not fill missing observations, generate forecasts or alter raw numeric values. Display formatting can round summary values; the history table provides numeric observations. Local retrieval dates are not observation years or release dates. An API dataset-level update date is not a claim that every indicator was revised on that date.

“Country” includes economies represented in the source; labels, classifications and boundaries follow the provider and do not express a political position.

Annual nominal GDP in current US dollars is not inflation-adjusted real GDP. GDP growth is an annual real-growth measure where defined by the source. Central-government debt is not general-government debt. Cash surplus/deficit is not interchangeable with the headline fiscal deficit reported by national governments. A missing external-debt series for a high-income economy does not mean zero debt. Governance estimates are statistical measures with uncertainty, not objective political ratings. Annual stock-market indicators are not current index prices, intraday charts, or investment recommendations.

## News

Publisher headlines, publication dates and external article links retain their publishers’ rights. The app links out for full coverage rather than bundling article bodies. Attribution is not independent verification of a publisher’s claims. RSS is a finite feed and may contain no matching country stories; it is not an exhaustive breaking-news or geopolitical-alert service. Distribution must comply with each publisher’s feed terms; review these terms before commercial launch.
